01

SFA System

Sales Force Automation · Client Project

A distribution business runs on information usually scattered across paper, memory and phone calls. This platform gathers it into one operational view: every representative, every outlet and every route across the territory, with live sales trajectory, stock movement and top product reporting, and role based administrative access backed by a complete audit trail.

  • Laravel
  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • REST API
  • Agile
02

NeuroMutation

AI and non AI attack detection and prevention

A security operations console that treats attribution as the first question rather than the last. It separates manual operators from automated tooling and autonomous agents, distributes every incident across five risk levels, and routes anything consequential into an approval queue instead of firing automatically. Detection spans an attribution engine, an immune layer and human escalation, with threat hunting, forensics and MITRE ATT&CK mapping alongside.

  • Python
  • Machine learning
  • Threat attribution
  • MITRE ATT&CK
  • Forensics
03

WP SSP Dashboard

Offline Capable Education System · Client Project

The Western Province Smart Student Portal was shaped by one demanding condition: the institution could not depend on the internet, so the system had to work without it. Registration, examinations and wellness checks run with no connection, while uploads, payments and email stay online and reconcile the moment it returns.

  • Offline capable
  • Progressive web app
  • Data sync
  • Dashboard design
04

Cyber Awareness Programme

Security Education · Community

Most breaches begin with a person rather than a protocol. This programme is delivered in person to Sri Lankan school students, covering digital footprint and privacy, the attacks they actually meet online, and the habits that prevent them. It is written in language students recognise and structured closer to a conversation than a compliance module.

  • Security awareness
  • Curriculum design
  • Workshop delivery
